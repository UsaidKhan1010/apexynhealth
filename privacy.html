Privacy Policy

Last updated: Nov 02, 2025

Summary

Apexyn (“we”, “us”) provides AI-based receptionist and automation services for clinics. We collect minimal data necessary to book demos and run pilots: names, emails, phone numbers, appointment details, and any information you provide during intake. We use Calendly for bookings, Zapier or Make for automation, and optionally Twilio for SMS reminders. These third parties may collect and process data under their own policies.

Data we collect

  • Contact data: name, email, phone number, organization.
  • Booking data: appointment date/time, notes you provide on booking.
  • Usage data: anonymized analytics data (Google Analytics) if you accept cookies.

How we use data

To schedule demos, run pilot automations, send confirmations and SMS reminders (if enabled), and to improve our website and service. We will NOT sell your personal data.

Third-party services

We may share data with service providers to operate (Calendly, Zapier, Twilio, Google Analytics). If handling PHI (protected health information), we will require a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) before processing. Do not send PHI through the demo booking or forms unless explicitly agreed in writing.

Cookies & analytics

We use cookies for site functionality and analytics. We only enable Google Analytics if you accept cookies via the cookie banner. You can opt-out anytime by clearing local storage and cookies for this site.

Security

We take standard industry measures to protect data. No system is perfect; if a breach occurs we will notify affected parties as required by law.

Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Contact

Questions? Email us at apexynhealth@gmail.com.

Note about HIPAA: Apexyn is not automatically HIPAA compliant by using third-party tools. If your clinic requires HIPAA-compliant handling, we will sign BAAs with the necessary providers and configure systems to minimize PHI exposure. Contact us to request HIPAA workflows before any pilot that involves PHI.
